# Age rating questionnaire

Select **None** / **No** for every content-frequency and capability question:

- Cartoon or fantasy violence
- Realistic or prolonged graphic violence
- Profanity or crude humor
- Mature or suggestive themes
- Horror or fear themes
- Medical or treatment information
- Alcohol, tobacco, or drug references
- Sexual content or nudity
- Contests, gambling, or simulated gambling
- Unrestricted web access
- User-generated content or messaging

The app is a household memory aid and contains no external content. Accept the
lowest rating App Store Connect calculates from these answers; do not manually
promise a specific badge because Apple's displayed age bands can vary by storefront.
