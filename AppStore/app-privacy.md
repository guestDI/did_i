# App Privacy answers

## App Store Connect questionnaire

- Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? **No**
- Tracking: **No**
- Data linked to the user: **None**
- Data not linked to the user: **None**

## Basis for these answers

Did I? has no account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, third-party SDK, backend,
or network request. Item names, timestamps, preferences, and an optional home
coordinate remain on the user's device. The App Group shares this data only with
the app's widget extension. Recent status is transferred only to the user's paired
Apple Watch. Apple-managed encrypted device backups may include local app data;
that does not make the data available to the developer.

Recheck these answers whenever data handling, dependencies, or network behavior
changes.
