## Description

Did I? gives future you a timestamp—not a guess.

Tap once when you switch off the stove, lock the door, unplug the iron, or finish another routine. Later, open the app or glance at a widget to see the latest current confirmation.

• Confirm common household checks in one tap
• See when each item was last confirmed
• Choose when a confirmation resets: after a custom duration, at your chosen daily time, when you come home, or manually
• Clear a current confirmation in one action
• Add widgets for quick confirmation and at-a-glance status
• Check recent confirmations from your Apple Watch

Green means a confirmation is current. Amber means there is no current confirmation. The app never uses alarming red states.

Private by design:
• No account
• No ads or analytics
• No developer server
• Your data stays on your devices

Location is optional. If you choose “When I come home,” Did I? uses an on-device region boundary to expire confirmations after you return. Your location is not sent to us.

Did I? is a memory aid, not a safety system. Always verify anything important directly.
