Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Did I? is designed so the developer does not receive your data. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, developer-operated server, or third-party SDK.

Data stored on your devices

The app stores your selected item labels, confirmation times and recent history, expiry rules, and preferences locally. If you enable “When I leave home,” it also stores a home coordinate locally. This information is not transmitted to the developer or sold or shared with third parties.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Apple's App Group mechanism shares local app data with the Did I? widget on the same device. Recent status can be sent through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to your paired Apple Watch. These transfers stay within your Apple devices.

Location and notifications

Location access is optional. When enabled, Apple location services monitor an on-device circular region so confirmations can expire after you leave home. The app does not upload or continuously display your location. Optional reminders are scheduled locally by iOS.

Backups and deletion

Apple-managed encrypted device or iCloud backups may contain local app data according to your Apple backup settings. You can remove Did I? data by deleting the app from your devices and, if desired, deleting associated device backups through Apple settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
